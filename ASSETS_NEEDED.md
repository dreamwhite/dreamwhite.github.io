The website is intentionally complete without inventing personal imagery. Replace these placeholders before the public launch:

| Asset | Recommended location | Intended use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Real portrait photograph | `public/images/portrait.png` | Home hero and About page — supplied and integrated |
| Brand logo | `public/images/logo.png` | Header mark, favicon, and browser identity — supplied and integrated |
| Printable CV PDF | `public/resume/dreamwhite-cv.pdf` | Resume download |
| Social preview image | `public/og-image.png` | Open Graph and social cards |
| Project screenshots and field photos | `public/images/projects/<slug>/` | Case studies — PRG32 repository media and user-provided hardware/demo photos are integrated |
| Architecture diagrams | `public/images/diagrams/<slug>/` | Project case studies |

Do not replace these with stock photography or generated faces. The current UI uses editorial placeholders until the real assets are available.
